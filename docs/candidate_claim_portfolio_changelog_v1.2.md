# Candidate portfolio v1.2 change log

Status: approved and frozen as immutable `1.2-final` by Royston on `2026-09-04`.

The immutable v1.1-final files are preserved under `framework/versions/v1.1-final/`. Version
1.2 is a new candidate version and does not retroactively modify that record.

- CAND-01, CAND-02, CAND-03, CAND-05, and CAND-06 were reframed as atomic organisational
  knowledge propositions. Policy identifiers were removed from exact wording and claim subjects.
- Their subjects, predicates, and objects now express the organisational rule being assessed.
- Policy identifiers remain only in administrative grounding hints and verification notes.
- Thresholds, roles, conditions, evidence targets, applicable periods, split, quantitative rules,
  and completeness records are unchanged.
- CAND-04, CAND-07, and CAND-08 exact wording is unchanged.
- Multi-condition approval propositions remain one atomic decision rule because threshold,
  authority, approval form, and timing jointly define one approval requirement. Evidence review
  must identify the precise component affected by any contradiction or qualification.
- Human Review now visibly separates **Claim being assessed** from **Evidence being reviewed**.
- After metadata-only normalization and schema validation, v1.2 was frozen as `1.2-final`.
- Separate blind reviewer packets were generated for Royston and Isanka. Held-out access remains
  sealed and final evaluation remains disabled.
