# Limitations and ethical considerations

The corpus is synthetic or anonymised. A small purposively chosen claim set cannot support
statistical generalisation. Reviewers and adjudicators make contestable research judgements;
their outputs are not unquestionable ground truth. Institutional authority depends on the
claim and context, and repeated statements may share one origin.

Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. The tool assesses whether supplied evidence
supports a precisely scoped claim; it does not assess whether an organisation or employee is
well managed, honest, or competent. Incomplete records, extraction errors, temporal gaps, and
informal practices can all change the appropriate interpretation.

The prototype must not be used for automated employment, disciplinary, legal, compliance,
or other high-impact decisions. Optional LLM wording assistance is not currently enabled; if
added later, it may introduce wording errors, must receive only structured records, must cite
every factual statement, and must never alter the deterministic outcome.
