# Four claim outcomes

- **Supported:** applicable, materially credible evidence supports the scoped claim and no
  material unresolved contradiction remains.
- **Contradicted:** applicable evidence provides a defensible basis for rejecting the claim
  within its scope and period, with no material support remaining.
- **Contested:** material supporting and contradicting evidence remains after applicability,
  time, version, and dependence checks. Policy-practice divergence commonly appears here.
- **Insufficient evidence:** the corpus does not justify any other outcome.

Qualifying evidence narrows scope or conditions; it is not a fifth outcome. Contextual
evidence aids interpretation but is not decisive by itself. Missing evidence creates a gap,
not an automatic contradiction. Confidence is always separate from the outcome.
